ABRAR ABIR

connecting dots & crossing lines

"abrar or abir?"
RSE @ Sidra Medicine
Computer Science @ CMU-Q '25

I’m a research software engineer (RSE) on Genomic Data Science Core at Sidra Medicine, which in practice means full-stack development on top of fragmented, high-stakes data (part of the work made it into a poster at SARF ‘26). Genomics is a big-data problem in its own right (on par with astronomy/social media) as sequencing gets accessible, yet more data hasn’t made drug discovery any more efficient (if anything, it’s gone the other way - following Moore’s law backwards!). Part of that is a tooling problem: life science doesn’t have a sustainable ecosystem for software development, so most tools get built for temporary scope then abandoned, and/or aren’t installable in a user friendly manner.

On top of it, life sciences demands a level of transparency and reproducibility that isn’t always straightforward. Plus, most of what I touch sits upstream of critical decisions at precision medicine or scientific discovery. All of it makes it so much fun compared to shipping boring tools for quarterly OKRs (let’s not talk about bullshit jobs).

On the side, I explore the wild landscape of open-source tools trying to shape conventional software engineering around the scope of life science. One such attempt is a desktop app for AlphaGenome I’m building with the molgenis folks at UMCG (a follow-up to my own pilot attempt), about making predictions accessible to non-technical users. Another scratch work is about trying to make LLM agent workflows robust and reproducible, chasing the determinism that genomics demands.

Not long ago, I had a good sprint with ML research. An NLP course pulled me into shared tasks (ArabicNLP ‘24, CLEF ‘24), and one thing led to another: a QNRF-funded CMUQ × QU collaboration on automated essay scoring for Arabic produced a new dataset (EACL ‘26). What stuck from that stretch was how SOTA methods built for Eurocentric languages start to fail outside the language family

Among other things, I’m (currently) curious about epigenomics × ML and agentic genomics. Downtime is lettering, chess, or hanging out in the bio/arXiv.

As usual, I’m open to collaborating on over-ambitious projects, discussing nearly-impossible ideas, or connecting over almost-thoughtful conversations.
